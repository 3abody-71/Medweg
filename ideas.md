# Medweg — Fidelity Reference

## Ground-Truth Spec

This permanent project is an implementation of the user’s existing **Medweg** website, not a new visual concept. The live website at `https://specpathway-heydt8xx.manus.space/` and the supplied React/Vite source are the visual and functional source of truth. The permanent copy must retain the calm, editorial medical-career guidance experience: a white canvas, deep teal-blue surfaces, warm gold accents, destination photography, compact information cards, and direct pathways from country exploration into individual detail pages.

## Chosen Design Direction: Clinical Atlas

**Design Movement:** Contemporary editorial wayfinding, informed by academic medical publications and an understated travel atlas.

**Core Principles:** Prioritize legibility before decoration; use clear hierarchy for complex pathway information; employ trustworthy clinical colors; keep card-based discovery practical and calm.

**Color Philosophy:** Deep teal-blue communicates clinical confidence and geographic exploration. Warm gold provides a restrained call-to-action accent, while paper-white and mist-gray preserve breathing space for dense factual content.

**Layout Paradigm:** A long-form map-and-card journey. The landing page begins with a broad orientation panel, moves into destination discovery, and then opens into country-specific briefings instead of forcing a dashboard-like experience.

**Signature Elements:** A compass-inspired brand mark; teal photographic overlays; small uppercase wayfinding labels with compact data cards.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Navigation must feel quick and predictable. Save controls, filters, and profile actions acknowledge intent without interrupting research.

**Animation:** Use short opacity and transform transitions only. Cards may lift very slightly on hover and content may enter with subtle staggering; no motion should distract from clinical information.

**Typography System:** A clean humanist sans-serif for body copy and a confident heavy sans-serif hierarchy for section titles. All-caps labels remain compact with deliberate letter spacing.

**Brand Essence:** A practical global medical-career atlas for graduates comparing international training routes. **Calm, credible, navigable.**

**Brand Voice:** Clear, candid, and guiding—not promotional. Example lines: “Find the country that fits your next medical chapter.” and “Verify the route, then make the next move with context.”

**Wordmark & Logo:** A compass-grid symbol alongside a compact Medweg wordmark. The mark should feel like a direction-finding instrument rather than a generic medical icon.

**Signature Brand Color:** Atlas Teal — `#0d7c9e`.

## Style Decisions

- Every page, including sparse utility pages, includes at least one Clinical Atlas cue: a route-orientation panel, compass reference, or practical guidance block.
- Specialty cards are framed as comparable training routes with compact factual cues, rather than generic category tiles.
- Empty states teach the next research action in the Medweg voice instead of merely reporting that no content exists.
